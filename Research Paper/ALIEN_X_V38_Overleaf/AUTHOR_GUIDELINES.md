# Author Guidelines

## Scope

The system-description paper must describe the system actually submitted to the BUBT Bengali NLP Safety Challenge 2026.

## Format

- A4 paper size
- Two-column layout
- 10 pt main text
- Recommended main-paper length: 4--6 pages
- References may be excluded from the page limit
- Appendix optional
- PDF submission generated from LaTeX/Overleaf

## Anonymous review

If anonymous review is used, select:

```latex
\usepackage[review]{bubtcompetition}
```

Do not include author names, affiliations, acknowledgments that reveal identity, or identifying repository links.

## Camera-ready

For the final version, select:

```latex
\usepackage[final]{bubtcompetition}
```

Include complete author names, affiliations, and contact information.

## Required scientific disclosures

Authors must report, where applicable:

1. Official and external datasets used.
2. Pretrained models and exact checkpoints.
3. Generative-AI or LLM/API use.
4. Data augmentation and synthetic data generation.
5. Manual rules or post-processing.
6. Ensembling.
7. Threshold tuning.
8. Hardware and software used.
9. Code/checkpoint availability.

## Academic integrity

Papers must be original and must accurately report performed experiments. Plagiarism, manipulated results, fabricated experiments, or undisclosed prohibited resources may lead to rejection or disqualification.

## Responsible reporting

Because the task concerns toxic and potentially harmful language, examples should be included only when scientifically necessary. Avoid exposing personal information and avoid unnecessary repetition of hateful, sexual, threatening, or abusive content.

## Suggested citation style

Use author-year citations consistently throughout the paper, similar to ACL-style NLP publications.
